# how to submit

1. Make sure your site is **live** at a public URL
2. Make sure your **Hackatime project** has at least 1 hour logged
3. [Fill out the submission form](/submit)

You'll need:
- Your name + email
- Your Hack Club Slack handle (find [here](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0159TSJVH8))
- Your GitHub repo
- Your Hackatime Project
- The demo URL of your site

# shipping an update

Kept building after your project got approved? Ship an update from the project's page on your [dashboard](/submit).

- Only **Hackatime time logged since your last approved ship** counts
- You need at least **2 hours** of new time
- Updates get reviewed just like a new project
- One update in review per project at a time

# rewards

Your **first approved project** comes with your luggage tag: its first 4 hours go toward the tag, and everything after that turns into stamps. Every project after that, and every update, turns **all** of its hours into stamps for the shop.
