# how can i contribute to the docs?

to contribute to these docs, write a `.mdx` file in this format:

```mdx
---
title: your page title
order: 10
---

# your page title

your content here
```

then **send your file to [@Alex Radu](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/team/U0AFWJX9CP2)** on Hack Club Slack.
